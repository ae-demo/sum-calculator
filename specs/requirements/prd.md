# Sum Calculator

## Problem Statement

People who want a quick, reliable sum of two numbers have to reach for a general-purpose calculator or spreadsheet. This product is a deliberately simple system that shows the full path from a screen to a back service and back.

## Solution

A web page where a user types two numbers and submits them; a back service adds the numbers and the page shows the answer.

## Actors

- User: a signed-in person who enters two numbers and sees their sum.

## Features

- F1 [Add two numbers](features/F1-add-two-numbers.md)

## Product-wide

See [Product-wide](product-wide.md).

## Out of Scope

- Operations other than addition (subtract, multiply, divide).
- Calculation history or saved results.
- A mobile app.
